What is a span?
\[ \text{Span}\{\mathbf{v}_1, \mathbf{v}_2\} = \{\, c_1 \mathbf{v}_1 + c_2 \mathbf{v}_2 \mid c_1, c_2 \in \mathbb{R} \,\}. \] In \( \mathbb{R}^2 \):
  • If \( \mathbf{v}_1, \mathbf{v}_2 \) are linearly independent (determinant \( \neq 0 \)), their span is all of \( \mathbb{R}^2 \).
  • If they are dependent (one is a scalar multiple of the other), the span is just the line through the origin in that direction.
