What is a span in ℝ³?
\[ \text{Span}\{\mathbf{v}_1, \mathbf{v}_2, \mathbf{v}_3\} = \{\, c_1 \mathbf{v}_1 + c_2 \mathbf{v}_2 + c_3 \mathbf{v}_3 \mid c_1, c_2, c_3 \in \mathbb{R} \,\}. \]
  • Rank 3 (determinant \( \neq 0 \)): the span is all of \( \mathbb{R}^3 \).
  • Rank 2: the span is a plane through the origin.
  • Rank 1: the span is a single line through the origin.
  • Rank 0: only the zero vector.
